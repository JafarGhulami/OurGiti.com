# Surface brief: home page (`/`)

## Scope and mode

The OurGiti home page. Visitor mode: **Persuade**. Build path: code-led (no image generation on this machine).

## Audience, job, action

- Owners, office managers and operations leads at organizations in Texas and across the US who need software built and/or IT infrastructure installed, supported and secured.
- Job: judge in seconds that OurGiti is a capable, professional partner for both software and IT, then get in touch.
- Action in working form: the contact form (name, work email, company, phone, services of interest, message). Sending needs OurGiti's email, which is pending: the send control says so plainly and the message can be copied meanwhile.
- Proof on hand: none beyond the four confirmed service lines. UI visuals are labeled illustrations; no clients, logos, figures, testimonials, certifications, hours or response times.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A premium-tech business site in the register of Stripe, Vercel and Cloudflare, executed straight and at full craft: confident sentence-case headlines, generous space, and product-style glass UI that shows the work, never stock photos. It refuses the previous notebook concept (graph paper, sketches, capitals, worksheet wording, lime).

OWN-WORLD: White and soft green-gray grounds by day, near-black green by night; one emerald brand green carries every action; frosted glass panels float over soft green light fields; one deep-green band per page; Mona Sans set tight, Geist Mono only for data inside the UI visuals; 16px-radius cards with hairline borders.

STORY: Visitors see one partner for software and IT, see each service as a working system, understand there is no gap between vendors, see how an engagement runs, and send a message.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Glass header (wordmark, section links, theme switch, "Contact us"; below md the links sit in a menu sheet). Left half: sentence-case headline in three lines at desktop, lead, primary "Request a consultation" and secondary "Explore services", service-area line. Right half: three glass panels over green light, layered so the two front panels (a resolved helpdesk ticket; backup and security) overlap the office-network panel and frost its rows; captioned as illustrative.

FORM: The category standard, chosen by the user in plain words after rejecting The Engineering Pad: "I didn't like the styles and looking and feel of this website it is not a professional bussiness website you need to redesign it". Benchmarks picked by the user: premium tech companies (Stripe, Vercel, Cloudflare); visuals: product-style UI built in code. No roll applies to this form.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Memorable moment

On load the hero's glass panels assemble: each starts visible with its fill but no frost, slightly low, then rises while its frost resolves, staggered, then rests. Cards lift slightly on hover. One orchestrated entrance, nothing scattered.

## Unresolved

- OurGiti's email, phone, address, city and hours (pending).
- Logo: none supplied; the wordmark is set type with a provisional mark.
- Process and positioning copy is drafted from the confirmed service lines and needs the company's review.
